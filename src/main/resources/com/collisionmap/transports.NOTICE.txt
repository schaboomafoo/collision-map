transports.tsv is derived from the transport tables of the Shortest Path plugin
https://github.com/Skretzo/shortest-path (src/main/resources/transports/transports.tsv and
agility_shortcuts.tsv). Rows that change plane or span more than 52 tiles were removed and the
columns were trimmed to origin, destination and skill requirement; those values are unchanged.
