# Collision Map

See where you can and can't walk. Collision Map outlines the walls around the area you're in, and marks the doors, gates and shortcuts that lead out of it.

## Features

* **See your walkable area:** Walls are drawn around everything you can walk to from where you're standing, so you can tell at a glance what's connected and what isn't.
* **Spot the way out:** Doors, gates, rocks and shortcuts are highlighted in their own color.
* **Look through doors:** Optionally preview the area behind each door, or several doors deep.
* **Mark objects:** Optionally outline and label doors, gates, ladders and other interactable objects, like `Gate (Open)`.
* **Smooth:** Built to run well even with a large draw radius, with or without the GPU plugin.

## Settings

Open the RuneLite configuration panel and search for **Collision Map**.

* **Draw radius:** How far around you to draw.
* **Wall color and thickness:** Make the outline match your style.
* **Look through doors:** How many doors deep to show. Set it to 0 to see only your own area.
* **Door color:** The color used for doors, gates and shortcuts.
* **Mark objects:** Turn on click box outlines and labels for interactable objects, and choose which actions count (Open, Climb, Enter and so on).
