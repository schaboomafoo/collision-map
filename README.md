# Collision Map

See where you can and can't walk. Collision Map outlines the walls around the area you're in, and marks the doors and gates that lead out of it.

## Features

* **See your walkable area:** Walls are drawn around everything you can walk to from where you're standing, so you can tell at a glance what's connected and what isn't.
* **Spot the way out:** Doors and gates are highlighted in their own color.
* **Nearby areas:** Areas just past a door, fence or river are outlined too, so you can see what's on the other side.
* **Mark objects:** Optionally outline and label doors, gates, ladders and other interactable objects, like `Gate (Open)`.
* **Smooth:** Built to run well even with a large draw radius, with or without the GPU plugin.

## Settings

Open the RuneLite configuration panel and search for **Collision Map**.

* **Draw radius:** How far around you to draw.
* **Wall color and thickness:** Make the outline match your style.
* **Nearby areas:** Also outline separate areas that start within this many tiles of yours. Set it to 0 to see only your own area.
* **Door color:** The color used for doors and gates.
* **Gate crossing lines:** Draw a dashed line across gates that take up a whole tile. Off by default.
* **Mark objects:** Turn on outlines (click box or tiles) and labels for interactable objects, and choose which actions count (Open, Climb, Enter and so on).
